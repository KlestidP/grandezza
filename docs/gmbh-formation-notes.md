# GmbH formation — Bremen reference notes

Personal reference notes for registering a GmbH in Bremen, kept here for later.
**Not legal or tax advice** — fees, thresholds, and requirements change; confirm
current figures with a notary, a Steuerberater (tax advisor), or Unternehmensservice
Bremen before acting on anything below.

## Rough process order

1. **Draft the articles of association** (Gesellschaftsvertrag). The
   Musterprotokoll template applies for a simple single-founder or up-to-three-founder
   setup with standard terms; anything more complex (custom profit split, vesting,
   multiple managing directors with different powers) needs a bespoke contract and a
   higher notary fee.
2. **Notarize the articles and appoint the managing director** at the same
   appointment — required by law, not optional. Bring ID and proof of the Bremen
   business address.
3. **Open a business bank account** in the company's name and pay in at least half
   of the €25,000 minimum share capital (€12,500) before the register filing; the
   bank issues a confirmation letter for the notary.
4. **Notary files with the Registergericht** electronically. Until this clears, the
   company is a "GmbH i.G." and founders carry personal liability for anything it does.
5. **Register the trade** (Gewerbeanmeldung) at the Gewerbemeldestelle once the
   register entry is through.
6. **File the tax questionnaire** (Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung) via
   ELSTER within a month of starting operations — generates the tax number and VAT ID.
7. **IHK (Handelskammer) membership is automatic** on trade registration — no
   separate application needed.

## Rough costs (verify current rates before relying on these)

- Notary, Musterprotokoll route: ≈ €320 + 19% VAT
- Notary, custom contract: ≈ €630+ (same capital)
- Commercial register entry: ≈ €300 (registration + retrieval fee)
- Trade registration: €32
- Share capital due upfront: €12,500 of the €25,000 minimum
- Ongoing bookkeeping (Steuerberater): commonly €80–200/month for a small GmbH

## Timeline

Roughly 5–6 weeks from first notary contact to a fully registered, operating GmbH,
assuming no delays.

## Bremen-specific note

Bremen's trade tax multiplier (Gewerbesteuer-Hebesatz) is 460%, above the national
average (~364%), and a GmbH gets no tax-free allowance on it (unlike a sole trader)
— it applies from the first euro of profit. Worth a conversation with a
Steuerberater before setting prices.
