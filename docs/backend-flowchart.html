How the two engines connect

One pipeline turns cold prospects into paying clients; a second builds their site and keeps their marketing running afterward. Both share a single platform spine and lean on the same handful of outside services.

The pivotal moment — lead becomes client
Read order: Acquisition runs top-left to right — source, score, draft, send, listen — until a reply becomes a booked deal. That single gold line is the only thing that crosses engines: it provisions a client record through the API Gateway and drops straight into Onboarding. From there Delivery launches the site (top row) and hands off into ongoing marketing, analytics, and the client's own dashboard (bottom row), which loops results back to the client. Both engines sit on one Postgres/Redis/S3 spine and call the same handful of outside APIs on the right.
